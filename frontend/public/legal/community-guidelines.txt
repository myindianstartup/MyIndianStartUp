Introduction
MyIndianStartup is a professional business and creator collaboration platform.
These Community Guidelines are designed to maintain a safe, professional, respectful, and trustworthy environment for all members.
All BusinessVerse and CreatorVerse members are expected to follow these guidelines at all times.
Violation of these guidelines may result in:
•	Content Removal
•	Profile Restrictions
•	Account Suspension
•	Permanent Account Termination
•	Membership Cancellation Without Refund
•	Reporting to Authorities where required
________________________________________
1. Maintain Professional Behaviour
Members must conduct themselves professionally and respectfully at all times.
Professional communication and ethical behaviour are expected throughout the Platform.
________________________________________
2. Respect Other Members
Treat all businesses, creators, professionals, and users with respect.
Harassment, bullying, intimidation, or abusive behaviour is not permitted.
________________________________________
3. Use Real Information
Members must provide accurate and truthful information.
Fake profiles, misleading information, and impersonation are prohibited.
________________________________________
4. Keep Content Professional
Content should be relevant to:
•	Business
•	Professional Services
•	Skills
•	Portfolio
•	Networking
•	Collaboration
•	Industry Discussions
________________________________________
5. One Post Per Day Rule
Members may publish:
•	One Image OR
•	One Video
per day.
Attempts to bypass posting limits may result in restrictions.
________________________________________
6. No Adult Content
The following are strictly prohibited:
•	Pornography
•	Adult Content (18+)
•	Nudity
•	Sexually Explicit Material
•	Escort Services
•	Sexual Solicitation
MyIndianStartup follows a zero-tolerance policy regarding such content.
________________________________________
7. No Illegal Activities
Users must not promote or engage in:
•	Fraud
•	Scams
•	Identity Theft
•	Financial Crimes
•	Cybercrime
•	Human Trafficking
•	Illegal Services
________________________________________
8. No Gambling Or Betting Content
Promotion of gambling, betting, lottery schemes, or similar activities is prohibited.
________________________________________
9. No Drug-Related Content
Promotion, sale, or distribution of illegal drugs, narcotics, or controlled substances is prohibited.
________________________________________
10. No Weapons Promotion
Promotion, sale, or marketing of prohibited weapons or dangerous items is not allowed.

11. No Hate Speech
Content targeting individuals or groups through hate, discrimination, or violence is prohibited.
________________________________________

•	Threaten Others

•	Stalk Others

13. No Spam

•	Repeated Promotional Content
•	Misleading Marketing
•	Mass Messaging
________________________________________

Only upload content that you own or have permission to use.

________________________________________
15. No Fake Reviews Or Testimonials
Members must not publish:
•	Fake Reviews

•	False Endorsements
