MyIndianStartup Dummy Accounts and Role Guide

This file documents local/demo credentials only. Do not use these passwords for production users.

1. Superadmin Account

Email:
superadmin@myindianstartup.test

Password:
SuperAdmin@123

Frontend dashboard:
http://localhost:3000/superadmin

Role:
Project owner / business owner level access.

What superadmin can do:
- View full platform overview.
- View total members, business profiles, creator profiles, posts, and storage indicators.
- View revenue-style subscription analytics.
- Manage or review admin-level access.
- See owner-level system status such as Supabase, R2, PostVerse rules, and admin layer.
- In production, this role should control sensitive settings and high-level business data.

How it works right now:
- The account exists in Supabase Auth.
- A matching member row exists in core.members.
- A matching role row exists in admin.users with role = superadmin.
- Frontend login validates this email and password through Supabase Auth.
- The frontend checks /api/admin/me and routes this role to /superadmin.
- Backend permission enforcement uses requireAdminRole('superadmin').

Role:
Platform manager / operations admin.

What admin can do:

- Review members and account activity.
- Monitor BusinessVerse and CreatorVerse profiles.
- Monitor PostVerse activity.
- Help manage support and moderation.

What admin should not do:
- Admin should not have owner-level control.
- Admin should not manage superadmin ownership settings.
- Admin should not access full business-owner revenue controls unless approved.

How it works right now:
- The account exists in Supabase Auth.
- A matching member row exists in core.members.
- A matching role row exists in admin.users with role = admin.
- Frontend login validates this email and password through Supabase Auth.
- The frontend checks /api/admin/me and routes this role to /admin.
- Backend allows this role to access /api/admin/overview and /api/admin/members.
- Backend blocks this role from /api/admin/superadmin.
